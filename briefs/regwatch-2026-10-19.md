## EBA classifies USDC as the first significant e-money token under MiCA, moving reserve, own funds and liquidity supervision from ACPR to the EBA on 12 November 2026

**EBA · EU · supervisory statement** · Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1506; MiCA significant EMT classification

Materiality: **High** (4.0) · actionability 4/5 · reach 4/5

Published 2026-10-14 · effective 2026-11-12 · application date 2026-11-12

*Who it catches:* crypto asset service providers, e money institutions, payment service providers, credit institutions  
*Whose desk:* compliance, financial crime, risk, finance, operations

The EBA classified USDC, issued in the EU by Circle Internet Financial Europe SAS, as a significant e-money token under MiCA, the first of the 45 e-money tokens notified in the EU to be classified significant. Dual supervision for significant EMTs applies from 12 November 2026: direct supervision of own funds, the reserve of assets, liquidity requirements and recovery and redemption plans moves from the French regulator ACPR to the EBA, while ACPR retains governance, qualifying holdings, fit and proper assessments, outsourcing and conflicts of interest. The EBA's page introduces that retained list with "such as", so it is not exhaustive and should not be restated as closed; the RegWatch edition of 19 October 2026 did restate it as closed, which is the error this note exists to stop propagating. The second-order consequence falls on firms that do not issue the token. A stablecoin whose reserve and redemption supervision sits with an EU-level authority is a different credit and liquidity proposition from one supervised by a single national regulator, so any token risk assessment or counterparty due diligence file recording ACPR as the prudential supervisor is wrong from 12 November. The split itself is the operational trap: reserve questions and governance questions now go to different authorities, and a firm escalating a redemption concern has to know which door it is knocking on. Scored actionability 4 rather than 5 because the obligation is binding and dated but the change most firms must make is to documents and escalation routes rather than to systems. Reach 4 as a defined set of licence categories holding, distributing, redeeming or quoting one token, not a whole sector. Recorded as a backfill; see window note.

**Action:** Before 12 November 2026, correct any token risk assessment, product approval or counterparty due diligence file that records ACPR as the prudential supervisor of USDC. Record which authority owns which question, and update the escalation route so a reserve, liquidity or redemption concern goes to the EBA and a governance, fit and proper or outsourcing concern goes to ACPR. Do not treat the retained list as exhaustive; the source hedges it.
