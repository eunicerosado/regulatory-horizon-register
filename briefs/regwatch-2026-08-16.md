## FinCEN final rule permanently removes beneficial ownership reporting for US companies and US persons under the Corporate Transparency Act

**FinCEN · US · rule final**

Materiality: **High** (5.0) · actionability 5/5 · reach 5/5

Published 2026-08-11 · effective 2026-08-14

*Who it catches:* all regulated firms, credit institutions, designated non financial businesses  
*Whose desk:* financial crime, onboarding, compliance, data

The final rule makes permanent the exemptions first introduced by the March 2025 interim final rule, narrowing "reporting company" to entities formed under foreign law and registered to do business in a US State or Tribal jurisdiction. US companies and US persons no longer report BOI; foreign reporting companies still report BOI for foreign individuals. FinCEN also announced it will delete BOI previously reported by US persons from the database. The second point is the one that reaches beyond US filers: an ownership data source that firms may have expected to corroborate against is being removed, not merely frozen. Verification of US entity ownership reverts entirely to firm-collected evidence. Note also that New York's LLC Transparency Act defines its terms by reference to the federal regime, so its scope narrowed alongside.

**Action:** Review CDD and onboarding policy wherever it references BOI filings or the FinCEN database as an ownership evidence source, and confirm what replaces it for US-formed entities. Applies to any firm onboarding US entities, not only US-regulated firms.
