## On 11 August 2026, FinCEN ended BOI reporting for US firms

**FinCEN · US · rule final** · Federal Register document 2026-16576, 91 FR 52508, Beneficial Ownership Information Reporting Requirement Revision, published 14 August 2026

Materiality: **High** (5.0) · actionability 5/5 · reach 5/5

Published 2026-08-11 · effective 2026-08-14

*Who it catches:* all regulated firms, credit institutions, designated non financial businesses  
*Whose desk:* financial crime, onboarding, compliance, data

FinCEN issued a final rule that permanently removes the requirement for US companies and US persons to report beneficial ownership information under the Corporate Transparency Act, effective on publication in the Federal Register. The Federal Register notice, read on 2026-11-19, gives publication and effect as 14 August 2026 and states that the rule adopts as final, with certain limited changes, the interim final rule issued on 26 March 2025; it also exempts reporting companies from submitting information about US person company applicants, and exempts US persons from updating information provided to obtain a FinCEN identifier. Foreign entities that are reporting companies must still report beneficial ownership information for foreign individuals. FinCEN also announced that it will delete previously reported information of US persons from the beneficial ownership information database. The second point is the one that reaches beyond US filers: an ownership data source that firms may have expected to corroborate against is being removed, not merely frozen, so verification of US entity ownership reverts entirely to firm-collected evidence. Verification on 2026-11-19 removed two claims that neither the release nor the notice supports: that the rule narrows "reporting company" to entities formed under foreign law and registered to do business in a US State or Tribal jurisdiction, and that New York's LLC Transparency Act narrowed alongside.

**Action:** Review CDD and onboarding policy wherever it references BOI filings or the FinCEN database as an ownership evidence source, and confirm what replaces it for US-formed entities. Applies to any firm onboarding US entities, not only US-regulated firms.
