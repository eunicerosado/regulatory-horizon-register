# Materiality rubric

Every item in the register is scored on two dimensions. The scores are
published alongside the item so that a reader can disagree with the
judgment on the record rather than guess at it.

Scoring is deliberately narrow. Two axes that can be applied consistently
week after week are worth more than five that drift.

---

## Axis 1 — Actionability

*Does an in-scope firm have to change something, and how soon?*

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 5 | Binding obligation with a dated deadline. Systems, policy or reporting must change. |
| 4 | Binding, but the implementation window is long or the change is contained to one function. |
| 3 | Supervisory expectation rather than rule change — firms are expected to be able to evidence a position. |
| 2 | Direction of travel is now visible. No action required, but planning assumptions should move. |
| 1 | Informational. Enforcement outcome, statistics, or restatement of existing obligations. |

## Axis 2 — Reach

*How much of the regulated population does it touch?*

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 5 | Cross-sector, or the whole of a major sector in a major jurisdiction. |
| 4 | A defined sector or licence category. |
| 3 | A sub-population — a threshold, a business model, a product type. |
| 2 | Narrow. A handful of firms, or a niche activity. |
| 1 | Firm-specific. Included only where the reasoning generalises. |

---

## Band

```
materiality = (actionability × 0.6) + (reach × 0.4)
```

| Band | Score | Treatment |
|------|-------|-----------|
| **High** | ≥ 4.0 | Leads the brief. Carried in the deadline calendar until closed. |
| **Medium** | 2.5 – 3.9 | Briefed. Tracked but not chased. |
| **Low** | < 2.5 | Recorded for continuity. Not briefed unless it moves. |

Actionability is weighted higher than reach on purpose. A dated obligation
affecting one licence category demands more of a compliance function than a
broad statement of direction affecting everyone.

---

## What is deliberately not scored here

Two further judgments govern whether an item becomes published commentary —
whether there is a genuine position to take, and whether it sits in a given
advisory lane. Those are editorial decisions and they are kept out of this
register, which records regulatory materiality only.

## Exclusions

Not recorded: personnel appointments, speech announcements without
substance, consultation closings with no published outcome, and items
already carried in a prior week unless they have materially moved (in which
case the new record cites the old one in `supersedes`).

## Limitations

This is a horizon-scanning register maintained by one practitioner from
freely available primary sources. It is not exhaustive, it is not legal
advice, and it should not be relied on as a compliance control. Items are
recorded as published; nothing here is an assurance that a firm's own
obligations have been correctly identified.
